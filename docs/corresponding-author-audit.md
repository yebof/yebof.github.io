# Corresponding-author PDF audit

Checked on 2026-11-26 against all 75 entries with a nonempty `pdf` field in `papers.bib`, including linked arXiv PDFs. All 75 PDFs were readable. Full-text searches were supplemented by author-block and footnote inspection; Springer envelope symbols were checked visually.

48 entries have an explicit designation (46 newly populated, 2 already populated). The other 27 retain no designation. This means no explicit designation was found in the checked PDF, not that the paper has no corresponding author. Entries without a PDF are outside this audit.

Only explicit corresponding-author wording or a correspondence symbol with an identified author was used. Affiliation symbols, equal-contribution notes, ordinary email lists, author order, and arXiv submitter information were not used as evidence. Names were validated against each BibTeX author list.
